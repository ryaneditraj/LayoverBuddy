About

A better way to read a connection.

Most flight sites stop at price and schedule. LayoverBuddy is for the hours in between: which airport you will stand in, how quickly you can reach the city, and whether the stop is long enough to be worth it.

The rankings use a sample dataset in this repository. They are a way to compare cities such as Singapore, Istanbul, Dubai, and Doha on a trip like Chennai to New York. They are not live airline results.
